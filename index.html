RESEARCH METHOD

Keep the receipts.

Current pages are not enough. Municipal history lives in versions.

  1. 1
    Capture the current site

    Preserve the live CivicPlus structure, alerts, page IDs and document links.

  2. 2
    Walk backward through Wayback

    Compare historical pages and URLs, including material that no longer appears in navigation.

  3. 3
    Pull underlying records

    Budgets, actuals, CIPs, personnel plans, ordinances, resolutions, contracts, council packets and audits. Use public City records and archived public copies first; PIRs are a fallback when the public trail ends.

  4. 4
    Normalize the money

    Connect fund → department → project → vendor → amount → fiscal year → source document.

  5. 5
    Show what changed

    Original plan, amendments, actual spending, delays, cancellations, cost changes and final result.

SOURCE VAULT

Primary records first

Official pages, PDFs and City archive collections are paired with Wayback history links. If the City ever published it publicly and Wayback preserved it, readers can inspect that history directly.
