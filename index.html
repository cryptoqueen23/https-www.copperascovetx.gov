RESEARCH METHOD

Keep the receipts.

Current pages are not enough. Municipal history lives in versions.

  1. 1
    Capture the current site

    Preserve the live CivicPlus structure, alerts, page IDs and document links.

  2. 2
    Walk backward through Wayback

    Compare historical pages and URLs, including material that no longer appears in navigation.

  3. 3
    Pull underlying records

    Budgets, actuals, CIPs, personnel plans, charters, ordinances, resolutions, contracts, votes, council packets and audits.

  4. 4
    Normalize the money

    Connect fund → department → project → vendor → amount → fiscal year → source document.

  5. 5
    Connect authority and vote

    Identify the charter provision, ordinance, council action, City Manager authority or other legal mechanism behind the decision.

  6. 6
    Show what changed

    Original plan, amendments, actual spending, votes, delays, cancellations, cost changes and final result.

SOURCE VAULT

Primary records first

Official pages, PDFs, City archive collections, charter records, ordinances, minutes and council packets are paired with Wayback history whenever available.
